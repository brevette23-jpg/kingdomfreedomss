How to use this planner

New here? Follow these steps in order. You can always come back to this page with the Help button.

1. Start with your dreams

The Dreams tab is where you write what you are working toward. Add a goal, set a target amount, and update how much you have saved. Tap Mark done when you reach it.

2. Plan your pay

  • Pay schedule: choose monthly, every 2 weeks, or weekly. For the last two, enter your first paycheck date.
  • Step 1: enter what you take home each paycheck.
  • Step 2: give each category a planned amount. Aim for nothing left unassigned.
  • Suggest 10% for giving fills in your giving amount for you.

3. Log purchases

In the Spend tab, enter an amount, pick a category, and add an optional note. Rough entries are fine. Tap Budget tool to read about budgeting methods and apply one to your plan.

4. See where you stand

The See tab shows what you have left to spend and a bar for each category. Gold means you are on track, amber means you are close to the limit, and rose means you are over.

5. Write in your journal

The Journal tab has three parts: thanksgiving, prayer, and reflection. Pick the date and add a verse if you like. Each entry is saved as its own page.

Moving between pay periods

The arrows at the top of Plan, Spend, and See move you to the previous or next pay period. Use Back to this period to return to today.

What the words mean

  • Planned: what you intend to spend in a category.
  • Spent: what you have logged in that category.
  • Unassigned: money you have not given a job yet.
  • Left to spend: your income minus everything you have logged.

Good to know

  • Your information is saved in this browser only. Using another browser or device starts fresh.
  • Removing an item asks you to tap twice, so nothing disappears by accident.
  • The light and dark button is under the title. Your choice is remembered.
